Westfield Outdoors

Building Westfield's Innovation Engine

Driving hero innovation through Strategic Brands while keeping Westfield a strong private label partner for retailers.

Innovation is the capability that lets Westfield consistently create differentiated products, strengthen Strategic Brands, and deliver proven value-driven innovation to retail partners.

01
Why it matters

Retail partners are asking for more than line extensions.

Innovation has to become a repeatable operating capability, not a one-off creative push.

Retail expectations

Retailers increasingly expect meaningful innovation that gives them a reason to reset, feature, and defend margin.

Consumer pull

Consumers reward products that solve real problems in clear, understandable ways.

Portfolio differentiation

Innovation differentiates both Strategic Brands and Private Label.

Competitive advantage

Westfield's advantage is product innovation, not advertising spend.

09
Operating capability

The process needs clear ownership and repeatable decision points.

Better tools only matter if the operating model reduces overlap and captures learning.

  • Clear roles and responsibilities with less overlap.
  • Portfolio planning that makes tradeoffs visible.
  • A decision framework for investment and launch readiness.
  • Continuous improvement after each development cycle.
  • Knowledge capture across teams, vendors, and product generations.
