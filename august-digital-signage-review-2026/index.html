GRAM Chair | What August showed

Innovation S28

A rocking chair with no rocker.

August argued that the existing frame could become the mechanism by replacing rigid corners with compliant ones.

1

Mechanism: eight elastomeric corners act as pivot and spring.

2

Advantage: no runners, coils, linkage bars, or hardware under the seat.

3

Physical test: samples were on the table so people could feel the motion and look underneath.

Suspend Chair | What August showed

SKW-044

Bring a swing. Not a chair.

August put the motion above the seat: the inner seat frame hangs from four straps and moves inside the folding outer frame.

1

Suspension: four static straps define the path while bungees control the ends of travel.

2

Experience: measured 5.6 in front-to-back travel with rocking layered into the swing.

3

Packability: no member added below the seat, so the outer frame still folds flat.

Suspend Chair | Current update

What changed after August

The motion is now the only job.

In development | S29
What Lia tested

Attachment points and seat width

A narrower stadium seat and new frame holes produced more motion and confirmed that attachment geometry materially changes the feel.

What we learned

Upper points alone are not enough

The next comparison must combine upper points, lower bungee locations, and strap length to reach controlled multi-axis motion.

Handoff condition

Document the best motion matrix

Lia owns the current action. Once the best configuration is recorded, Max can accept the handoff and translate it into specific V3 CAD.

We are deliberately not polishing the chair shape yet. First lock the attachment geometry that gives controlled swing and sway; then design V3 around the winning motion.
