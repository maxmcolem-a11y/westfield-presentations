How this trip differs

Connect me to the work.
Keep the arrangements flexible.

A solo, flexible working visit

I came alone, not with a group. I declined a driver and special meal arrangements; factory dining lets me eat when the work allows, not at a fixed lunch time.

I move between office, factory and projects as samples need attention—without coordinating an entourage.

Introductions, then direct contact

Hengfeng office support makes the right connections. I get the maker’s WeChat, stand beside them and exchange messages while we work. A full-time translator or minder adds coordination and limits that mobility.

Working: Instant Indoor + Tactical Messenger

Direct access to the people doing the work made immediate follow-up and rapid iteration possible.

Still missing: Flow Motion V2

Cyrus is doing a good job, but I still lack direct WeChat contact with the frame builders and fabric makers. Routing everything through him recreates the same narrow channel I have from Indianapolis.

Connect me directly so I can move person to person and place to place as the sample progresses.

Optional enhancement: a translator available by WeChat video call for difficult conversations—not a problem encountered or a prerequisite for this approach.
