03 / 21

Operating Philosophy

People + Process + AI

AI works only when it is paired with good people and disciplined process.

People

Experience, creativity, relationships, judgment, leadership, taste, and final business ownership.

Process

Repeatable workflows, clear ownership, good data discipline, decision history, and accountability.

AI

Speed, memory, summarization, monitoring, pattern recognition, comparison, recommendations, and drafting.

LLMs

Language engines like ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Llama that read, write, summarize, compare, and reason across information.

Agents

Workflow systems with a role, approved information access, and repeatable jobs: checking, preparing, watching, and supporting.

Important limit

AI is not automatically correct, does not fix bad process by itself, and should not make final business decisions alone.

06 / 21

Sales / PM Proof Point

Chuck Is a Practical PM/Sales AI Pilot Already Working

Matt's Chuck prototype shows the Sales / PM lane in practical form: AI-powered presentation building for line review workflows.

The problem

Buyer presentations can take hours of copying from Trello, finding images, checking specs, adding pricing, and formatting slides.

What Chuck does

Reads Trello boards directly, pulls card data, product photos, specs, pricing, buyer feedback, comments, and attachment URLs.

The output

Builds a complete HTML buyer presentation from a natural-language request and publishes a live URL for review or presentation.

Why this matters

PMs get a faster first pass, more consistent line-review formatting, and more time for commercial judgment and account strategy.

07 / 21

Chuck Workflow

From Trello Board to Buyer Presentation

Chuck turns the PM workflow into a repeatable pipeline instead of a manual copy-paste exercise.

1. Ask

A PM asks for a deck in natural language: "Build the Walmart 27 line review deck."

2. Pull

Chuck reads the Trello board, lists, cards, comments, attachment URLs, product names, specs, pricing, and buyer notes.

3. Build

Product slides include real photos, structured product information, pricing, comments, and account-ready storytelling.

4. Deploy

The deck publishes as a live URL that can be shared quickly, reviewed internally, and revised on the fly.

This is the Sales / PM counterpart to the Industrial Design proof point: AI supporting real work that is already happening.

Success Standard and Guardrails

A Good System Produces Clear, Useful Outputs That People Trust Enough to Use

Success should be measured by business effectiveness, not by how many AI tools are purchased.

What good looks like

Accurate enough to trust, simple enough to use, visible enough to manage, flexible enough to grow, disciplined enough to scale, and human-led.

What not to do

Do not buy tools without workflow, build disconnected experiments, skip human review, expect AI to fix unclear ownership, or connect sensitive information without governance.

Business metrics

Faster risk visibility, more complete line-review prep, faster buyer deck creation, fewer missed decisions, better account memory, and stronger executive visibility.
