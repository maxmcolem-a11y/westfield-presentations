The pull rule

A handoff completes when the next owner takes the folder.

Moving the card is not the transfer. Acceptance is.

1 · Pack the resultOutput and open risks go into the folder.
2 · Work travels; Current Owner remainsThe supplier work moves. The named Westfield Current Owner still holds the folder.
3 · The next owner takes itAcceptance rotates the Current Owner, action, and date.

Fig. 6 — Westfield development · HengFeng sampling · Westfield acceptance

Why pull? Work that must be taken cannot be dumped invisibly. Until the next owner is ready, the Current Owner still holds it and the waiting state remains visible.

The company boundary: The folder always belongs to Westfield. HengFeng may perform the sample work; the named Westfield Current Owner remains responsible through the request, follow-up, review, and acceptance.

S28 evidence method

What the snapshot measures — and what it does not.

Scope

All 199 open cards across all 19 open Innovation S28 lists, captured Aug. 2, 2026. Strictly read-only.

Due date

Six of 199 cards have Trello’s built-in due date populated: 3%. S28 does not distinguish an action date from a milestone.

Department / executor queue

Cards in Sample Room, HengFeng, Westfield functional, or Outside Design queues. This measures board organization, not the performance of a function.

Assigned member

At least one visible Trello member. A proxy for visible ownership, not proof of the S29 Current Owner contract.

Interpretation boundary

The analysis evaluates S28 as a portfolio operating system — never the performance of a person or function.

Held for Q&A

48% lack a member; 71 of 74 HengFeng or Sample Room cards lack a due date; 27% are inactive at least 30 days.
