01 / 11

The familiar starting point

Every product already creates a folder full of work.

Briefs, sketches, CAD, samples, tests, decisions, and lessons already belong together. What changed is how that record carries responsibility as the work moves.

Physical era · record and work traveled together

Where the folder sat, ownership sat.

  • One folder accumulated the product's evidence and decisions.
  • It moved desk to desk with the current piece of work.
  • Its location showed who owed the next move and where it went afterward.

Digital era · the record became shared

Access spread faster than accountability.

  • Evidence moved into drives, chat, email, and boards.
  • Everyone could reach the work without anyone visibly holding it.
  • Ownership became something reconstructed from meetings, messages, and memory.

S29 is not a return to paper. It keeps shared digital access and restores one visible holder, one next result, and one date.

What S29 changes

For S29, every product gets one persistent folder—from idea to launch.

So this season starts by reconnecting the record: one master card keeps the evidence, decisions, and history together while responsibility moves.

  • The brief, concept evidence, specifications, samples, tests, decisions, and retrospectives accumulate in one record.
  • The folder moves through durable product-maturity states; the card is not recreated for each phase or sample round.
  • Execution teams keep their detailed tools. Links point downward without creating a second authoritative project record.
  • Launched, parked, and cancelled folders retain the learning for the next season.

The accountable handoff

A handoff is complete when the next person takes the folder.

When the current result is finished, responsibility moves only when the next person explicitly accepts it.

Fig. 7 — Westfield development → HengFeng sampling → Westfield review

HengFeng may build the sample, but a named internal owner holds the folder through the request, follow-up, review, and acceptance of what comes back.

The human discipline

Leadership decides whether the board becomes truth—or paperwork.

That chain stays reliable only when leaders resolve status on the board. Accept reconstructed status elsewhere, and the folder becomes optional.

EveryoneUse plain language on the folder. No tokens, jargon, or new ceremonies.
Portfolio Owner / PMOwn the product identity, overall accountability, target milestone, and intended path through release.
Current OwnerKeep the current result, date, output, open risks, and Blocked Reason true.
Next OwnerTake the folder explicitly — one acceptance comment.
Development approverPersonally record one decision — APPROVED FOR DEVELOPMENT — at one gate.
Leadership — all of usRun every portfolio review from the board. Resolve missing status by fixing the folder in the room.

Use the board to make decisions, and keeping it current becomes the work—not extra work.

08 / 11

The agentic layer

Real portfolio discipline, without adding a project-management layer.

Once the discipline is clear, agents make it sustainable: they absorb the routing and memory burden that paper offices assigned to clerical staff. People decide.

Fig. 8 — The services point, gather, and explain · People retain every decision

The steward

Walks the shelf on schedule, flags objective gaps and missed commitments, and keeps advisory clarity questions private until they prove useful.

Personal service

Gives each participant a short brief: folders held, folders arriving, what changed — and updates only on explicit request.

Leadership service

Surfaces the decision queue, milestone risk, and portfolio load without reconstructing status in the room.

Agents never invent owners, dates, acceptance, approvals, priorities, or business decisions. The board remains fully usable with every agent switched off.
