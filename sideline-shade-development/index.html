The problem

Every shade starts with a puzzle.

Sort the polesWhich section goes where, in what order, and is one missing?
Thread the sleevesPush 4 m of pole through fabric without it snagging or splitting apart.
Clip, bend, adjustBow the poles into place, then fiddle it square — usually with two people.

We took the puzzle out. All of it.

The idea

Lay it flat.
Pull.
It stops.

  1. 01Spread it out. The poles are already sewn into the fabric, in loose short sections.
  2. 02Pull the orange cord. Every joint snaps shut (too small to see from here) and the frame goes stiff.
  3. 03Keep pulling. The green doorway straps draw each arch's feet together, and the arches rise.
  4. 04The straps' sewn stops hit their rings: it stops at the right shape, and stays.

What you get

A doorway of shade. One cord.

Two arches with canvas between them. No loose poles, no clips, nothing left over in the bag.

Taking it down

Down in seconds. Stuffed, not rolled.

The problemIt has to come down as easily as it went up, and pack without care.
The ideaRelease the one-way catch: every joint springs open, the arches fall flat, and the whole thing gets stuffed in a sack.

The difference

Instant up. Easy down.
