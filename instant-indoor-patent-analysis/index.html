Executive Summary

Key Findings

  • Burrow, Inc. holds the most directly threatening patent portfolio — 40+ patents covering modular sofa construction with tool-free lever clip assembly (US10,932,580 and family), closely overlapping with Instant Indoor's core concept.
  • Lovesac holds 28+ Sactionals patents (US7,213,885 family) covering modular furniture with interlocking connector systems — relevant but focused on cushion-based modularity rather than steel tube frames.
  • A clear design-around path exists if Westfield differentiates on its steel tube folding/locking mechanism (adapted from outdoor chairs) vs. competitors' wood/panel-based modular systems.
  • Westfield has strong own-IP to build on — its folding chair patent portfolio (US10,213,023, US11,109,682, US9,930,966 etc.) provides a foundation for extending steel tube locking mechanisms to indoor sofa-scale furniture.
  • Immediate patent filings recommended for the steel-tube-to-sofa adaptation, the modular tube interlock system, and the flat-fold sofa geometry — all have MODERATE to STRONG novelty.
