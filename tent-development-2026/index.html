A possible development approach

Explore form and function in parallel.

Form + user space

Printed volumes, scale figures and full-size space checks help us compare:

  • A distinctive, recognizable silhouette
  • Useful headroom, entry and interior space
  • Panel language and plausible construction

Deployment + performance

Physical trials would help the team evaluate:

  • User actions and operating effort
  • Stability, release and compact repacking
  • Durability and weather performance
Printed forms guide design choices. Physical trials establish performance.

A conventional-pole structure could provide a useful comparison alongside the new mechanisms.

Easy deployment and compact pack-down remain the product requirements.05 / 07
