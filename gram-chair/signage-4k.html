02 — The idea

The frame is the mechanism.

Eight elastomeric corners replace the rigid elbows. Under a weight shift the side profile shears square to parallelogram — that geometry change is the rocking arc, and the strain stored in the corners puts the chair back.

04 — It is a real mechanism

Square to parallelogram, solved.

All eight corners flex by the same angle θ. They restore with 8·k·θ while gravity pushes the other way at m·g·L per radian — in this kinematics gravity is destabilising, so the corners supply every bit of the centring.

07 — Proof · it holds

It rocks, it centres, and it stays inside its allowables.

At 250 lb the recommended corner self-centres with 2.6× margin, peaks at 14% strain against a 50% static allowable, and cycles at 8% against 20%. At 150 lb the margin is 4.3×. Seat sag under load stays under 3 mm.

09 — Where it goes

One side. One tool. Two materials.
